# Nassau-Candy-Distributor-Data-Analytics-Project
Objective

The goal of this project was to perform an end-to-end exploratory data analysis (EDA)
on the sales and distribution records of Nassau Candy Distributor, a U.S.-based
confectionery company. The analysis covers two full fiscal years — January 2024 to
December 2025 — and was carried out as part of a Data Analytics Internship (2025–2026).
The project aimed to:

Understand revenue trends, growth drivers, and seasonality patterns
Evaluate product-level profitability and identify underperforming SKUs
Analyse regional and geographic distribution of sales
Assess customer behaviour and billing patterns
Deliver findings through multiple output formats for different audiences


About the Dataset
The dataset contains 10,194 order line items from Nassau Candy Distributor's internal
sales system. It spans 24 months across the United States with the following scope:

Total Records        : 10,194 order line items
Unique Orders        : 8,549
Products             : 15 SKUs across 3 divisions (Chocolate, Other, Sugar)
Unique Customers     : 5,044
Cities Covered       : 542 across 59 U.S. states and territories
Data Completeness    : 100% — zero missing values across all 20 columns
Date Range           : January 2024 to December 2025

Key columns include Order ID, Product Name, Division, Region, State, City,
Sales, Gross Profit, Cost, Units, Ship Mode, Customer ID, and Order Date.

Key Findings
Revenue and Growth

Total revenue over two years: $141,784
Gross profit: $93,443 at a margin of 65.9%
Revenue grew by 44.6% year-over-year (2024: $57,956 to 2025: $83,827)
Margin remained stable across both years, confirming volume-driven growth

Product Performance

Wonka Bars (5 flavours) account for 78.8% of total revenue at $111,692
Highest margin product: Everlasting Gobstopper at 80% — only 13 units sold
Lowest margin product: Kazookles at 7.7% gross margin — a value-destroying SKU
The Chocolate division alone drives 92.9% of all revenue

Regional Performance

Pacific region leads with $46,302 (32.7% of total revenue)
Atlantic follows at $41,197 (29.1%)
Gulf region is significantly underpenetrated at only 15.7% share
California is the top state contributing 19.7% of national revenue alone

Seasonality

Q4 (October to December) is the peak season in both years
December 2025 reached $12,338 — nearly six times January 2024 sales
All years show consistent acceleration from Q3 into Q4

Customer and Operations

Average order value: $16.58 with a median of $13.00
Average items per order: 1.19 — most orders are single-product
Standard Class shipping handles 60% of all orders
All shipping modes maintain the same ~66% gross margin


Tools and Technologies Used

Python 3 — core programming language
Pandas — data loading, cleaning, and aggregation
Matplotlib and Seaborn — data visualisation and chart generation
Jupyter Notebook — interactive analysis and dashboard
HTML, CSS, and Chart.js — live interactive web dashboard
